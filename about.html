هدفنا

About us

Kora Stats is a free site for following football results in the top European leagues, the Egyptian and Saudi leagues and more, with goals and match events.

How it works

Results are collected automatically from public data sources and refreshed about every two hours. They are not live and can lag behind a match by up to two hours.

Our goal

A simple, fast and clear page on mobile and desktop, in Arabic and English, with light and dark modes.
